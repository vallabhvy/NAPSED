#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment**: Unbelievably high specificity. The application genuinely feels like a piece of high-precision Braun industrial test equipment. The physical bevels, recessed cavities, and tactile status lenses perfectly enforce the "proof over pedigree" product philosophy. It is impossible to mistake this for a generic SaaS dashboard.

**Deterministic Scan**: The detector found 23 issues. Almost all of them are `design-system-font-size` violations where arbitrary tiny sizes (`text-[11px]`, `text-[10px]`, `text-[9px]`) are used instead of following the typography ramp in `DESIGN.md`.

#### Overall Impression
The visual aesthetic is absolutely masterful and perfectly matches the brand. However, the typography execution creates a massive "wall of text" that causes cognitive overload, and the lack of power-user shortcuts betrays the engineering audience. 

#### What is Working
1. **The Materiality**: The deep carbon dark mode with precise inset shadows (`.chassis-plate`, `.recessed-meter`) is flawlessly executed.
2. **The Status Lenses**: Using radial gradients to create glowing physical LEDs (`status-lens-emerald`) grounds the digital UI in physical reality.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Visual Noise Floor via ALL CAPS**: Every label, button, header, and paragraph is ALL CAPS `text-xs tracking-wider`. When everything screams, nothing stands out.
  - **Why it matters**: Severe eye fatigue. It violates the "Aesthetic and Minimalist Design" heuristic by giving equal weight to secondary context text and primary metadata.
  - **Fix**: Revert long-form paragraphs (like incident context) to sentence case. Reserve the all-caps tracking exclusively for metadata, tabs, and buttons.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`
- **[P2] Undocumented Micro-Typography**: 23 instances of `text-[11px]`, `text-[10px]`, etc. scattered throughout the code.
  - **Why it matters**: It fragments the design system and makes global scaling impossible.
  - **Fix**: Standardize a `--text-micro` (11px) and `--text-nano` (10px) token in the design system and refactor the arbitrary Tailwind classes.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable extract`
- **[P2] Missing Power-User Accelerators**: An app for senior engineers ("Proof over pedigree") only has one keyboard shortcut (`⌘K` for search).
  - **Why it matters**: Engineers hate clicking through forms. It slows down the core "Operate" loop.
  - **Fix**: Add `⌘Enter` to publish forms, `Esc` to dismiss modals/return to feed, and visual `<kbd>` tags on primary actions.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`
