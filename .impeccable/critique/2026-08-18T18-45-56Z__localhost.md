## 1. Executive Summary

The application has successfully integrated the required functionalist changes without compromising its core identity. By addressing the cognitive overload and interaction friction, the design has matured from a visually striking prototype into a production-ready engineering tool.

### Resolved Issues
- **[RESOLVED] Visual Noise Floor**: The "Wall of Text" has been addressed. By adjusting opacities (`text-theme-ink/60`) and adding 1px demarcations, the visual hierarchy is clear while maintaining the strict ALL-CAPS constraint.
- **[RESOLVED] Undocumented Micro-Typography**: The 23 instances of arbitrary utility classes (`text-[11px]`, etc.) were systematically extracted into formal design tokens (`--text-micro`, `--text-nano`, `--text-pico`).
- **[RESOLVED] Missing Power-User Accelerators**: Core workflows are now fully keyboard-accessible (`⌘Enter` to publish, `Esc` to escape deeper views, `⌘1`/`⌘2` for tabs) and exposed via tactile `<kbd>` badges.
- **[RESOLVED] Missing Depth Cues**: Modals (like the Command Palette and Notification Drawer) now utilize `backdrop-blur-sm` to physically separate them from the complex UI underneath, completing the physical materiality metaphor.
